# Mimir beta: a guide for testers

Thank you for testing Mimir. It's a catalogue and discovery engine for films, TV series, books, games and music
that finds works by how they feel, explains every pick, and links you to where you can watch, read, play or
listen.

## What's in it

- **503 works**: 171 films, 67 TV series, 115 books, 66 games and 84 albums, from 28 original languages
  (including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Korean, Japanese, Mandarin, Spanish, French and Arabic).
- **128 tags** in a controlled vocabulary (genre, theme, mood, pacing, complexity, narrative, world, style), each
  with a definition, and a parents' guide in six categories that distinguishes "none" from "not assessed".
- **Open-data facts** from Wikidata, Open Library and MusicBrainz where a source matches: years, runtimes, page
  counts, critic and audience scores (Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, IMDb as recorded on Wikidata), box office,
  book and album covers, track lists. Each page says where each fact came from.

## Things to try

1. **Ask in your own words** on the home page: "a Korean drama that will make me cry", "something like Breaking
   Bad", "a short, funny Hindi film for the family", "a TV series I can finish in a weekend", "an album for
   focusing, calm and minimal", "a book like Outer Wilds". Open "Why this fits" on any pick.
2. **Tell Mimir what you love.** React to a few works (♥ loved, ✓ liked, ✕ not for me), then open
   **For you**: picks grouped by "Because you loved …", with the qualities they share. Try the medium tabs.
3. **Browse like a catalogue.** The menu has a tree per medium (Films, TV, Books, Games, Music) with search
   pages: an A–Z bar, a content filter (click a tag once to include it, twice to exclude it), rankings, decades
   and time ranges.
4. **Wander the shelves**: Laugh Riot, Drama Mamas, Nepo Babies, Desi Delights, Seoul Searching, Binge-Worthy,
   Side Quests, Page Turners, Rap Sheet, Hush Hour and more.
5. **Pick your apps.** On any work page, "Where to watch / listen / read / play" lets you choose your service
   (Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, Spotify, Apple Music, Kindle, Steam …) and region. Mimir remembers them.
6. **Check the parents' guide** on a few works you know well. Is the level right? Is anything "not assessed"
   that should be?

## What we want to hear

Use **Send beta feedback** (footer, or Catalogue → Send beta feedback) or **Report a problem with this page** on
any work. Both open an email to hello@terratalesmedia.world with the page address filled in. Most useful:

- A request where the picks were wrong, and what you expected instead.
- A tag, summary, fact or parents'-guide level that is wrong.
- A "where to watch" link that goes nowhere useful in your region.
- Works or whole areas you expected to find and didn't ("suggest a title" appears on empty searches).
- Anything confusing in the navigation.

## Good to know

- Your ratings, shelf, preferred apps and region are stored **in your browser only**. Clearing site data resets
  them. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you email us.
- Tags, summaries and parents' guide levels are **AI-assisted readings awaiting editorial review**, and are
  labelled as such. Facts without a source note are unverified.
- Links to streaming and music services **open a search** on that service; Mimir can't yet confirm what's
  available in your country.
- Films, TV and games show **generated covers** until we have licensed posters; books and albums show their real
  covers where Open Library or the Cover Art Archive has them.
