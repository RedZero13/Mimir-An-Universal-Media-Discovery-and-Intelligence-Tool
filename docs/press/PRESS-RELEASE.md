## Terratales launches Mimir, a free discovery engine that finds films, shows, books, games and music by how you want to feel

*Describe a mood, a favourite or how much time you have; Mimir picks from about 7,500 works across six media and tells
you why each one fits.*

**New Delhi, October 2026.** Terratales Media Entertainment today opened Mimir to the public at
[mimir.terratalesmedia.world](https://mimir.terratalesmedia.world/). Mimir is a free catalogue and discovery
engine that brings films, TV series, books and manga, games, albums and YouTube into one place, and answers the
question every streaming queue, bookshelf and game backlog raises: what next?

Instead of a search box that matches titles, Mimir takes a request in plain words: "something comforting after
a difficult day, no violence", "a book like Outer Wilds", "a game I can finish in one evening", "I want to die
laughing watching a show". It shows how it read the request, then returns a handful of picks, each with the
reasons it fits, drawn from recorded facts and clearly labelled editorial readings.

**What Mimir does**

- **One catalogue for every medium.** About 8,300 works from 100 countries in 68 original languages, from Hollywood,
  Bollywood, Korean drama and anime to novels, manga, indie games, classic albums and YouTube creators. 681 are
  fully classified against 372 controlled terms, including 233 subgenres; the rest are being classified in batches.
- **Picks that explain themselves.** Every recommendation says why it fits, and whether each reason is a fact or
  an interpretation.
- **Built around your time.** Every work carries how long it takes, from a quick bite to a long haul, including
  real audiobook lengths for 88 books.
- **A parents' guide for every medium**, telling "none" apart from "not assessed".
- **A library that keeps you going:** watch, read, play and listen rings, about 60 badges, custom trackers,
  shareable lists and a year in review.
- **Watching together:** watch-alongs with comments that stay hidden until everyone reaches that moment, plus
  reviews, follows and seasonal challenges such as Spooktober and Noirvember.
- **Private by default.** A visitor's library lives in their own browser unless they choose to sign in. Mimir
  needs no app store; it installs from the browser on phones and computers.

Mimir runs its discovery engine without a large language model: requests are interpreted into inspectable
constraints, ranked against a classified catalogue, and explained from evidence. Facts come from open data such as
Wikidata, Open Library and MusicBrainz, and every page says which source each fact came from.

Mimir is in public beta. The catalogue grows every week, and feedback from early users decides what comes next.
